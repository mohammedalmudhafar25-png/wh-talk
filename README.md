# WH Talk — MYALM Education

Interactive speaking games for English learners:

- 🎲 **Dice Interview** — grow a short answer into a BIG answer with the WH tools
- 💬 **Dialogue Match** — choose the right reply, then act out the chat
- 🎧 **Lesson 10 — That's About It!** — tap-to-listen phrases in American or British English
- 🚢 **Shipped to Africa (Madagascar)** — listening & speaking games, character chats, shadowing and skill reports: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/shipped-to-africa/
- 🧭 **A1 · Lesson 1 — Directions & Time: Find Your Way** — 10 games (place, movement, at/in/on, clocks, direction words, map quest, taxi dialogues, role-play cards, explain my way) with UK/US voices and speaking checks: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/a1-lesson1-directions/ · 🎧 Listening pack (all lesson audio, UK & US): https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/a1-lesson1-directions/audio.html
- ☕ **A1 · Lesson 2 — Mike & Tom: Let's Go Out!** — present simple through a café and restaurant story: 5 stops (read the story with Arabic, listening detective, café words and writing, speak up with role-plays and ordering food, my favorite place) with UK/US voices, volume and speed sliders, speaking checks, saved progress and voice recordings: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/a1-lesson2-lets-go-out/
- ⚓ **Port Talk (warm-up, Marine Service / AGP)** — dialogues at the port, True/False, explain why, talk about your work: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/port-talk-warm-up/
- 🍭 **Ralph & Vanellope Meet (A2 · L7)** — Wreck-It Ralph movie lesson: words, listening line by line, gap-fill, team race, discussion and dubbing, with Mr. Mohammed's voice: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/ralph-and-vanellope-meet/
- ♟️ **Walter Meets Gus (B1+ · L8)** — Breaking Bad movie lesson on the whole scene: element word cards, vocabulary match, listening line by line, gap-fill, true/false, Speak Like Walter phrases, pitch role-play, translation game, Stone film narration, team showdown, discussion and dubbing, with UK/US voices and Mr. Mohammed's own voice for every instruction: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/walter-meets-gus/
- ☀️ **The Green Field · 2036 (last lecture, B2→C1)** — Ali, Mohanad and Muayad meet again as strangers when Basra's fields run on solar power: a dramatic story (listen with or without text, tap any word for UK/US pronunciation and Arabic), Genuine or Fake dinner game, C1 phrases, shadowing, role-play and free conversation cards with roles and a timer, plus a free speed slider, saved progress and voice recordings: https://mohammedalmudhafar25-png.github.io/wh-talk/three-journeys/
